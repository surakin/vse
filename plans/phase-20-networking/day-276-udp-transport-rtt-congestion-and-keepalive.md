# Phase 20 / Day 276 — UDP Transport III: RTT, Congestion Control, Keep-alive & Timeouts

**Phase:** [Phase 20 — Networking](../phase-20-networking.md)
**Depends on:** Days 274-275.

Making the UDP transport a good network citizen: it must not flood a path, and it must notice when the other side is gone.

## Tasks

- [x] **RTT estimation** (smoothed RTT and variance from acked packets, Karn's rule for retransmitted packets), exposed in the statistics; resend timeout derived from it with a floor and ceiling.
- [x] **Congestion control:** a conservative, documented window-based algorithm (NewReno-style: slow start, congestion avoidance, halve on loss, reset on timeout) with pacing so a burst is not sent at once; an application cap (`net.max_bandwidth_bytes_per_second`) on top; the send queue reports backpressure as the window closes; unreliable messages are dropped (oldest first) before reliable ones are delayed. The algorithm is written down in the spec with its parameters and the reasoning.
- [x] **Keep-alive and timeout:** an empty acknowledged packet when idle, a connection timeout, and the first-ack-after-silence rule; path MTU fixed at 1200 payload bytes with an optional probe left out (recorded).
- [x] **Statistics:** per-connection RTT, jitter, loss, throughput, window, queue depth, retransmissions, duplicates, drops by reason.
- [x] **Tests** (`tests/net/udp_congestion_test.cpp`): over the simulated network with a 1 Mbit/s, 40 ms path the throughput converges within 10 percent of the cap without sustained queue growth; two flows share a bottleneck within a factor of 2; loss bursts recover; the connection survives a 5-second blackout shorter than the timeout and closes with `TimedOut` after a longer one; RTT estimate within 10 percent over a jittery path; a deterministic regression trace pins the algorithm.

## Outcome (2026-11-12, Linux machine)

- Deviations from the task text: the "first-ack-after-silence rule" is not implemented (empty packets are never answered, to avoid ping-pong; each side's own keep-alives and acknowledgements show the peer is alive); `net.max_bandwidth_bytes_per_second` is `UdpTransportConfig::max_bandwidth_bytes_per_second` until the settings of Day 281; the two-flow test uses two connections of one client transport (the simulated network gives each endpoint pair its own link).
- Algorithm (written out in `docs/guide/net.md`, "The UDP transport: round trip, congestion control and giving up"): NewReno-style window in bytes (initial 10 packets, minimum 2), slow start and congestion avoidance only while the window is at least half full, one cut per round trip, time-out to the minimum window, pacing at 1.25 x window / srtt with a 4-packet burst, an application cap as a second token bucket; time-out 1.5 x srtt + 4 x variance, loss by three newer acknowledgements only after 1.25 x srtt + 4 x variance; an unreliable message discards the oldest queued unreliable frames when the queue is full.
- Also: a client that closes during the handshake sends `Disconnect` with id 0 and its salt, so the server frees the connection at once (the Day 274 entry in `KNOWN-ISSUES.md` is removed). Statistics: `jitter_seconds`, `send_rate_bytes_per_second`, `window_bytes`, `bytes_in_flight`; `UdpConnectionInfo::{window_cuts, timeouts_fired, dropped_unreliable}`.
- Measured (`tests/net/udp_congestion_test.cpp`, 10 cases): 1 Mbit/s, 40 ms, 20 KB queue: about 98 percent of the cap with a Reno sawtooth; two connections within a factor of two; blackouts of 5 s survived and a longer one ends in `TimedOut`; RTT within 10 percent; the cap within 10 percent. At 60 percent random loss the window stays at its minimum, so the conformance and 1 MiB tests allow simulated hours (the simulation costs seconds).
- Runs: plain `ran 2768 of 7336 tests (phases: all; flavors: plain; display: included; crash tests: excluded)` (all passed), asan `ran 2190 of 7336 tests (phases: all; flavors: asan; display: included; crash tests: excluded)` (one flaky benchmark failed, passed alone; filed), tsan `ran 1866 of 7336 tests (phases: all; flavors: tsan; display: included; crash tests: excluded)` (only the known scene test failed); the UDP and simulated-network tests also pass on GCC, Clang non-unity and an optimized tree; `docgen lint` clean, docs 7 of 7.

## Post-implementation code review

`/code-review high`: 7 findings, 5 fixed, 2 declined. Fixed: a round-trip sample from an acknowledgement that was itself lost for seconds inflated srtt and the pacing rate (test `unreliable traffic lost in a blackout ...` fails without the clamp, checked); packets of unreliable frames could pin the window shut (now every counted packet times out; the test passes either way, so this one is covered by reading, not by a failing test); a time-out too tight while the bottleneck queue filled (throughput 82 to 98 percent of the cap); unbounded pacing debt and a needlessly drained cap bucket; a quadratic scan when dropping unreliable frames. Declined: no undo of the window cut after a spurious loss (standard behavior); the first-ack-after-silence rule (above).
