## Notes

- `spatial/spatial_debug.{h,cpp}`: `DescribeSet` (`SpatialStats`: item counts, static and dynamic tree nodes, depth and height, SAH cost against the cost after the last build, moves, re-inserts, memory), `AppendBoxLines`, `DebugLines` (nodes, items, one query volume, hits; capped by `max_lines`), `RegisterSpatialSettings` (`spatial.parallel`, `spatial.fat_margin`, `spatial.stats`, `spatial.debug_draw`, each with env and flag names), `SetConfigFromSettings`. `SpatialWorld::UseSettings` makes `Parallel()` follow `spatial.parallel` at run time; `settings::Setting::IsBound` added for it. Tests `tests/spatial/debug_test.cpp`.
- Found by the benchmark and fixed: `PairTracker::Update` gathered neighbours with `SpatialSet::Traverse`, which scans every item of a grid, so the update on a grid was quadratic (100,000 items: 28 s). It now uses the new `SpatialSet::ForEachHandleInRegion` (the grid's cells; `ForEachInRegion` is built on it): 55 ms. The existing "same events with a tree and with a grid" test covers the result.
- Benchmark (`tests/spatial/spatial_bench.cpp`, hidden `[.][spatial-bench]`, Release, Ryzen 9 7950X3D): results and the comparison with the pass condition are in the design spec, section 4.1. Met: culling (80x at 100k, 95x at 1M), queries (overlap 0.41 us, ray 0.64 us at 100k), moved object at 1 percent (0.70 us). **Missed: 100,000 all-moving agents** (ECS sync 156 ms against 80 ms, and 1.0x with 8 workers against 2x): the moves are applied on one thread and the tree re-inserts ~0.85 us per move in each of the two sets. Profiled (instrumentation, removed), the cost is the dynamic tree's re-insert. Options for the user: (a) a grid for the physics set (moves 8x cheaper), (b) a default margin of 0.5 (tree moves 71 ms against 83 ms for 100,000), (c) apply the moves of the independent sets as parallel jobs (up to 2x with 2 sets, none with 1), (d) keep the numbers as they are. Filed in KNOWN-ISSUES.md and to be presented at the phase close (Day 249).
- Demo: `game/spatial_demo` runs 50,000 entities (a fifth static blocks, the rest moving agents) through the sync with a culling query, the physics set's contact count, a sweeping ray pick and a radius query each frame, `--stats`, `--debug-draw`, `--frames`, `--entities`, `--serial`; ctest `spatial_demo_runs`. Reduced from the task text: it is headless (no window, no golden image, no README/settings gate, no `--backend`), because a rendered version needs a line pass in the renderer and an ECS-driven scene, which no phase has planned; the lines are counted, not drawn. It is for the user to say where the rendered demo belongs. Filed in KNOWN-ISSUES.md.
- Deferred from Day 247 and still open: the `renderer.culling` setting (the renderer's settings registry is a separate system; `RenderScene::SetCullMode` is the run-time switch), light culling through the tree.
- Runs: plain `ran 2190 of 5713 tests (phases: all; flavors: plain; display: included; crash tests: excluded)` (the two known X11 window failures), asan `ran 1672 of 5713 tests` clean, tsan `ran 1354 of 5713 tests` (the one known scene abort). `ctest -L docs` 7 of 7, docs 7675/7675, lint at zero. Clang and GCC non-unity clean.

## Post-implementation code review

Nine low-severity findings, no correctness, lifetime or thread-safety bug.
- **Fixed (3):** the query volume and its hits were the first lines dropped at `max_lines`: they are now drawn first (test `crowded`, fails without the fix); a comment at the pair tracker's region scan states the invariant it depends on (margin at most `largest_margin_`); a negative or absurd `--entities`/`--frames` in the demo is replaced by the default.
- **Declined (6):** a grid's "nodes" are its item boxes (cosmetic, a grid has no node boxes; items=false avoids the double); `items_visited` on a grid now counts items that fail `exact` (statistics only, no test compares it); `SetParallel` ignored while a setting is used (documented); `spatial.stats` and `spatial.debug_draw` are for programs that draw or time, the demo keeps its launch-only flags (allow-listed); `max_lines` below 12 yields no lines (it is a cap); the `phases.txt` line order (harmless).
