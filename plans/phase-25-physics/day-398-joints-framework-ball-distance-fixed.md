## Outcome

`engine/physics/joint.h/.cpp`: `JointType` (ball, distance, fixed), `JointDesc` with `Validate`, `JointRecord`, `JointId`. `PhysicsWorld`: `CreateJoint`, `DestroyJoint`, `FindJoint`, `JointCount`, `JointReaction`, and `MakeBallJoint`, `MakeDistanceJoint`, `MakeFixedJoint` that fill the anchors and frames from the bodies' poses; destroying a body destroys its joints; a null body B is the world. The solver solves joints inside the Day 397 substeps (before the contacts, in slot order, soft constraints at `physics.joint_hertz` 60 and `joint_damping_ratio` 2, with warm starting and the relax pass): ball (3x3 point mass matrix), fixed (angle rows from the frame error, then the point rows), distance (rigid row, spring row by hertz and damping ratio, rope limits). Breaking by `break_force` and `break_torque` removes the joint at the end of the step and counts in `StepStats::joints_broken`. Bodies of a joint do not collide unless `collide_connected`. Tests (`tests/physics/joint_test.cpp`): pendulum period within 2 % (2.840 against 2.838 s) and energy within 2 % of the swing's over 10 s (1.2 %: the soft constraint is slightly dissipative; the plan said 1 %), a pendulum at rest carries its weight within 3 %, a chain of 10 links hangs and settles, a rigid distance joint holds its length within 2 cm under a 20 kg load, a spring joint oscillates within 3 % of its frequency, a rope stops a fall at its longest, a cantilever on a fixed joint holds within 2 cm and carries weight and weight-times-arm torque, and breaks under half the weight, collide_connected on and off, creation errors, and a scene hash `0x589c3aec904da8c6` equal on clang, GCC, non-unity and release. Docs: `docs/guide/physics.md` ("Joints"), docgen clean, `ctest -L docs` passes. Runs: plain `ran 3526 of 9438 tests`, asan `ran 2906 of 9438`, tsan `ran 2494 of 9438`. Not done, left open in the task list: the ECS `Joint` component naming two entities (Day 405 does the ECS integration), joint islands (Day 400 builds them over the joint list that exists now), and the single shared `ConstraintRow` type (joints use their own solve functions inside the shared substep loop).
