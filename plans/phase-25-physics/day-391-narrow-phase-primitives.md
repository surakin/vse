# Phase 25 / Day 391 — Narrow Phase I: Spheres, Capsules and Sphere-Box

**Phase:** [Phase 25 — Physics](../phase-25-physics.md)
**Depends on:** Day 390.

Contact generation for the pairs that have closed forms, with one manifold type for everything.

## Tasks

- [x] `ContactManifold`: normal (from A to B), up to 4 points each with position on A, position on B, penetration depth and a `feature_id` (stable across steps for warm starting), plus the pair's combined friction and restitution.
- [x] Analytic tests: sphere-sphere, sphere-capsule, capsule-capsule (closest points of two segments, parallel and degenerate cases handled without division by a near-zero), sphere-box (closest point on the box, the centre-inside case picks the least-penetration face).
- [x] Each test returns a manifold or none, is symmetric (swapping the arguments flips the normal and the ids consistently), and uses only `+ - * / Sqrt`.
- [x] A dispatch table `Collide(shapeA, xformA, shapeB, xformB, manifold*)` keyed by the shape pair, with a documented slot for every pair of shapes (unimplemented ones return `Unsupported` until their day).
- [x] Speculative margin: contacts are generated up to a small distance (`physics.contact_margin`, built as an advanced setting and `StepConfig::contact_margin`) so a resting contact is stable and an approaching one is predicted.
- [x] Tests: per pair, analytic scenes (touching, penetrating by known depths, separated, concentric, parallel capsules, capsule end-to-end, sphere inside box) with the expected normal and depth within 1e-5; a randomised symmetry test (10^5 pairs, `Collide(A,B)` equals the flipped `Collide(B,A)`); a property test that moving B by the returned depth along the normal separates the pair.

## Outcome

Built: `physics/contact.h` (`Pose`, `ContactPoint`, `ContactManifold` with `Flip`, `CollideResult`, `CombineMaterials`) and `physics/collide.h/.cpp` (`Collide`, the dispatch with a documented cell per pair of kinds). Tests with closed forms: sphere-sphere, sphere-capsule (the ball of the capsule's radius at the closest axis point), capsule-capsule (clamped closest points of two segments, degenerate segments included; parallel axes give a two-point manifold at the ends of the shared stretch; crossing axes through one point use the common perpendicular), sphere-box (in the box's frame: face, edge and vertex regions as feature ids, a centre inside leaves through the face of least penetration). The margin gives speculative contacts with negative depth: `physics.contact_margin` (0.02 m), also in `StepConfig`. Box-box and capsule-box answer `Unsupported` (Day 392).
Checks: analytic scenes per pair (touching, penetrating by known depths, apart, speculative, concentric, parallel, end to end, collinear, crossing, rotated box, inside, on the surface), each within 1e-5; 100,000 random pairs of all kinds: swapping the arguments gives the flipped manifold (points matched by position, tolerances 2e-3 on the normal and 5e-4 on positions and depth: near-parallel capsules lose a few ulps), every point's depth equals `(position_a - position_b) . normal`, and the normals are unit; a separation property on 50,000 random pairs (moving B by the depth along the normal separates them). **Cross-compiler determinism:** the outputs of 100,000 random pairs hash to `00ff613a34e7890e` under clang and GCC, unity and not, Debug and Release (committed). The first sample differed under GCC, which was the test's, not the code's: a call whose arguments draw random numbers (`Vec3(rng.Range(), rng.Range(), rng.Range())`) evaluates them in the compiler's order (GCC right to left, Clang left to right); the helpers now draw into locals, and the broad-phase and step tests that do the same are noted below.
**Deviations:** (1) box-box, capsule-box and hulls are `Unsupported` as planned. (2) The symmetric test compares sets of points (the two ends of a parallel stretch come in either order). (3) `broad_phase_test.cpp` and `step_test.cpp` still build vectors from argument lists that draw random numbers, so their scenes differ between GCC and Clang; their assertions (oracle equality, the committed step hash, which draws no randoms) hold either way, but the broad-phase scenes should be drawn into locals if a hash of them is ever committed.
**Runs:** plain `ran 3455 of 9155 tests (phases: all; flavors: plain; display: included; crash tests: excluded)`, asan `ran 2765 of 9155 ...`, tsan `ran 2423 of 9155 ...`, no failure; clang, GCC, non-unity and Release `physics_tests` 73 of 73; `ctest -L docs` 8 of 8, docgen lint at zero for `physics`. Not run: the shared-library and importer-off builds, the Windows and macOS machines.
