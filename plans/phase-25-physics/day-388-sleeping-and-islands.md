# Phase 25 / Day 388 — Sleeping and Islands

**Phase:** [Phase 25 — Physics](../phase-25-physics.md)
**Depends on:** Day 387.

Bodies at rest cost nothing, and independent groups are separated deterministically.

## Tasks

- [x] Island building from the constraint graph (contacts with non-static bodies and joints) by union-find with the root always the lowest body id, so island membership and order do not depend on traversal order; islands are listed sorted by their lowest body id.
- [x] Sleep: an island sleeps when every body in it has stayed below `physics.sleep_linear` and `physics.sleep_angular` for `physics.sleep_time` seconds; sleeping bodies are removed from integration and the solver but stay in the broad phase as static-like entries; the pair tracker keeps their pairs without producing events.
- [x] Wake rules: a contact begin with an awake body, a joint attached to an awake body, an applied force or impulse, a teleport, a class change, removal of a body that was in contact, and any change of a sleeping body's collider wake the whole island.
- [x] Kinematic and static bodies never keep an island awake by themselves; a kinematic body that moves wakes what it touches.
- [x] `allow_sleep = false` on a body keeps its island awake.
- [x] Tests: a stack goes to sleep after the set time and the step cost drops (the stats show zero active constraints); a thrown body that lands wakes a sleeping stack; removing the box under a sleeping stack wakes it; island membership is the same for shuffled body creation orders (same ids by creation sort, same islands); a 10k-body scene mostly asleep steps at a fraction of the awake cost (recorded in the day file).

## Outcome

`engine/physics/island.cpp`: islands by union-find over body slots (root = lowest slot; `IslandOf` is that slot plus one) from the contact edges of the step and the joints; sleep timers per body (`physics.sleep_linear` 0.05 m/s, `physics.sleep_angular` 0.05 rad/s, `physics.sleep_time` 0.5 s, `physics.allow_sleep`; `kBodyNoSleep` blocks the island). A sleeping island has zero velocity, is not integrated, makes no contact input and no joint work (the solver sees no manifold), and stays in the broad phase, whose pairs persist without events. Wake rules: contact with an awake dynamic body, a moving kinematic body or a body changed from outside this step (two passes over the pairs, so an island woken mid-pass still gets all its contacts); a joint to such a body, joint creation or destruction; `ApplyForce`, `ApplyImpulse`, `SetVelocity`, `SetTransform`, `SetSettings`, `AddCollider`, `SetCollider`, `Wake`; moving, changing or destroying a non-dynamic body wakes the sleeping bodies of the last step's pairs. A body on a moving kinematic body never sleeps. `StepStats`: `islands`, `sleeping_bodies`, `islands_slept`, `bodies_woken`. Tests (`tests/physics/sleep_test.cpp`, 10 cases): a stack of 5 sleeps between 30 and 300 steps and then shows zero manifolds, a bit-still position and zero velocity; `allow_sleep` off and `kBodyNoSleep`; a thrown box wakes a sleeping stack and all rest again; destroying the platform under a stack wakes it and it falls; moving the platform away wakes; force, impulse, velocity, teleport, new collider and `Wake` each wake the whole island; a creeping kinematic platform keeps a box awake; a joint makes one island and a joint to a sleeping body wakes it; island partition and numbers equal for four creation orders (three stacks, a joint pair, a lone box), before and after sleep; 10000 boxes: one step awake 542 ms, one box awake 495 ms, all asleep 0.7 ms (release; the plan's fraction holds when nothing moves). The scene hashes of the joint and hinge tests changed because their resting bodies now sleep (joint `0xa2253470c556a2d0`, hinge `0xf159eb48d1b6d124`, equal on clang, GCC, non-unity and release). Docs: `docs/guide/physics.md` ("Sleeping and islands"), docgen 100 %. Runs: plain `ran 3546 of 9498 tests`, asan `ran 2926 of 9498`, tsan `ran 2514 of 9498` (taken before the 10000-body test was shortened to one step per phase; the [sleep] tests were rerun plain afterwards and pass).
