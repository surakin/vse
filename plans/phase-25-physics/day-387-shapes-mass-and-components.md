## Outcome

Built: `engine/physics/{validation,shapes,mass,components}.{h,cpp}`. `Shape` is one tagged value (sphere, box, capsule along Y) with `Validate()`, `Bounds(position, rotation)` and `LocalHalfExtents()`; `MassFromDensity` gives volume, mass, centre and the inertia as principal moments (closed forms; the capsule's hemispheres by the parallel axis theorem), `InverseInertiaWorld` is two products, `InertiaAbout` is the parallel axis shift; `RigidBody`, `Velocity`, `Collider` and an inline `PhysicsMaterial` with `CombineRule` (the pair's rule is the one with the larger number, so the order of the colliders never matters), each with a `Validate()` returning a `Validation` value (host data is refused, never aborted on).
Checks: sphere and box against analytic values (1e-5 relative); the capsule against a 120^3 grid integral (1.5%) and against the sphere when its cylinder is empty; a rotated box's world inverse inertia (a quarter turn swaps the entries; `I^-1 (I w) = w` about a skew axis); bounds of three shapes under four rotations contain 1681 surface points each and are tight within 6%.
**Decisions and deferrals** (the plan said otherwise in three places): (1) the `PMAT` minted material asset is **not built**: the material is inline in the `Collider` (the C API and the minters have no need until a game asks for shared materials); `KNOWN-ISSUES.md` lists it. (2) The convex-hull mass (tetrahedron decomposition) is **Day 393**, where the hull exists. (3) Components are plain structs; their registration with the storage layer (Phase 19) comes with the world on Day 388, where the entity set is decided. (4) Hull, mesh and compound are not shape kinds yet.
**Runs:** plain `ran 3406 of 9008 tests (phases: all; flavors: plain; display: included; crash tests: excluded)`, asan `ran 2716 of 9008 ...`, tsan `ran 2374 of 9008 ...`, no failure; GCC (`build-gcc`) and non-unity (`build-nounity`) `physics_tests` 25 of 25; `ctest -L docs` 8 of 8, docgen lint at zero for `physics`. Not run: the shared-library and importer-off builds, the Windows and macOS machines.
