## Outcome

The spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-16-physics-design.md` holds the decisions and the six spikes; the spike sources are `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-16-physics-spikes/` (as `.txt`, so the formatter and the gates leave them alone). Every plan default held:
sequential impulse with warm starting (3.4 to 4 times less penetration and zero jitter, against 2 to 13 cm/s of jitter for position based; a cold start costs 6 times the penetration), a 4-point manifold with feature ids (a 1-point manifold never settles), 60 Hz with 10 iterations (30 to 120 Hz stable), the `StaticBvh` and a `PairTracker` margin of 0.1. The determinism spike showed which rules matter: contraction off, no fast-math and ordered merges give one hash across GCC and Clang, `-O0` to `-O3` and 1 to 32 threads; FMA contraction (`-march=native` default), fast-math and an unordered merge each change it.
Two findings changed days: (1) a world must call `RebuildStatic()` after any static change, or the pending statics are scanned by every query (31 ms against 4 ms a frame on 2000 statics and 5000 moving boxes): Day 377 gets a test for it; (2) `PairTracker::Update` is single-threaded and costs 4.7 ms for 5000 moving boxes: Day 400 uses that figure as its reference and may parallelise the gather as a Phase 18 extension.
Plan edits made: Day 374 (flag test covers clang-cl and notes arm64), Day 377 (the `RebuildStatic` test), Day 400 (the pair-feed reference).
**Runs:** `scripts/test-phase.sh --affected` (plain): `ran 3380 of 8934 tests (phases: all; flavors: plain; display: included; crash tests: excluded)`, 3380 passed (it runs every phase because `CURRENT_PHASE` changed); `ctest -L docs` 8 of 8. **Not run:** the asan and tsan flavors (the day adds no code, only text and a phase number), the other machines (no shared code changed besides `CURRENT_PHASE`, which the PC and the Mac read on their next pull).
Limits of the spikes are in the spec (translation-only stacks, a plane for the tilted cube, spheres for the determinism hash, a single thread for the pair feed).
