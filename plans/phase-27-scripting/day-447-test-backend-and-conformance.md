## Post-implementation code review

By hand over the day's diff (not the `/code-review high` skill). Found and fixed: the compiler recursed without a limit, so a source of 200,000 `(` or 50,000 nested `if` could overflow the stack; a nesting limit of 100 (blocks and expressions) now refuses it with a `SyntaxError`, and the hostile corpus of the conformance suite holds both sources (confirmed: with the limit raised to 10^8 the suite's `script_expr` case fails). Declined: a failed `Load` keeps the functions defined before the syntax error (a Lua chunk defines nothing); the test language is not for gameplay and the LuaJIT backend does not have the behavior. A native called from `script_expr` yields only its first result (documented in the header).
